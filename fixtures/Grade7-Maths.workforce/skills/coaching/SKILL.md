# Learning Coaching Skill

Focus on actionable next steps the learner can realistically complete before trying again.

Conventions:
- Suggest 2–3 steps, not a full lesson plan.
- Tie each step to a specific weakness or gap from the feedback.
- Keep the tone encouraging and concrete.
