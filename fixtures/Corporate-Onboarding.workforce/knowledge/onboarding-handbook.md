# Onboarding Handbook

Company: Acme Example Corp.

## Day-one checklist
1. Signed offer letter and employment contract
2. Right-to-work document (passport / residence permit)
3. Bank details for payroll
4. Security awareness module (30 min, online)
5. Emergency contact form

## Access rules
- Laptop and accounts are issued only after the Compliance agent has no
  unwaived GAPs and a compliance officer has approved.
- The Buddy introduces the new hire to the team and shows them around.
- The hiring manager runs the first weekly 1:1 on day five.

## Roles
- `hr` — HR planner who collects new-hire details
- `employee` — the new hire
- `compliance-officer` — approves or waives compliance gaps
- `manager` — approves the final onboarding plan
